Resistor Addition (addition series)

Fundamentals

When connecting components, in this case resistors in series, the total equivalent resistance is the sum of all individual resistors, meaning they simply add together. In this configuration, the resistors carry exactly the same current but generate different voltage drops based on their individual resistance values.
https://www.electronics-tutorials.ws/resistor/res_3.html (information source, Wikipedia)

Uses

Resistors in series are useful to decreasre the voltage (while keeping the current the same) to a safe value for other components like LED's.

Formulas Rearrangements

The formula can be rearranged to find the total resistance or an individual resistance value. The rearrangements are as follows:

  • RT = R1 + R2 + R3 + ... + Rn
  • R1 = RT - (R2 + R2 - ... - Rn)
